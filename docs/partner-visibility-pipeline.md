# Partner visibility pipeline

Last updated: 2026-08-25

Purpose: track whether an active Miloosh affiliate/referral relationship also creates a realistic path for the vendor to expose Miloosh to its own audience. Affiliate approval by itself is **not** treated as reciprocal promotion.

## Highest-value paths

| Partner | Miloosh relationship | Reciprocal visibility path | Current action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pipedrive | Active affiliate | Official affiliate tiers say Growth affiliates are eligible for co-marketing initiatives. Growth requires 2-5 sales/month for at least 6 consecutive months. Partner directory appears to belong to solution providers, not ordinary affiliates. | Asked Pipedrive Affiliate Team on 2026-08-25 for exact co-marketing criteria, publisher spotlight opportunities, and directory eligibility. |
| Constant Contact | Active affiliate | Separate Creator Partner Program advertises creator-directory exposure plus speaking and co-marketing opportunities. | Asked affiliates@constantcontact.com on 2026-08-25 whether Miloosh can join Creator in parallel without breaking current affiliate attribution. |
| GetResponse | Active affiliate | Official program: Gold tier at 100+ sales in 12 months includes co-marketing opportunities and a personalized landing page. | Treat as earned visibility target. Do not assume access before Gold. |
| Wrike | Active referral partner | Wrike has a public partner directory, but current directory is oriented to resellers, consultancies, service partners and systems integrators. Referral program page does not promise a listing. | Asked Wrike Partners Team on 2026-08-25 whether referral publishers can receive directory profiles, partner spotlights, communications exposure or co-marketing. |

## Separate partner programs, not benefits of the current affiliate approval

| Partner | Public visibility mechanism | Why it is not automatic for Miloosh |
|---|---|---|
| monday.com | Certified partner marketplace/directory | Public profiles are for certified solution partners. Current Miloosh relationship is an affiliate/referral relationship, not a certified implementation practice. |
| Airtable | Services Partner Directory and Airtable sales/marketing support | Requires Services Partner accreditation, training/testing and services capability. |
| Omnisend | Agency directory, lead access and co-marketing | These are Agency Partner benefits; Omnisend's affiliate program is a separate publisher program. |
| Wix | Wix Marketplace | Requires Wix Partner Program participation and Icon-level eligibility (2,000 points) before Marketplace application. Affiliate approval alone is not enough. |
| Shopify | Shopify Partner Directory | Directory is for service partners who provide merchant services; Shopify Affiliate Program is separate. |
| MailerLite | Integration directory | Directory listing is for integration partners that build a MailerLite integration, not ordinary affiliates. |
| Hubstaff | Integration/API co-marketing | Hubstaff promotes integrations and API partners; ordinary affiliate approval does not create this benefit. |
| SurveyMonkey | App/integration and strategic partnership paths | Affiliate program is separate; strategic partnerships require a separate proposal. |

## Explicitly unavailable

- **Close:** Close confirmed on 2026-08-24 that content publishers are not eligible for the Close Partner Directory. The active referral relationship remains valid.

## No reciprocal exposure currently verified

No current first-party evidence has been recorded that ordinary affiliate approval alone provides Miloosh with vendor-side publication, directory placement, newsletter exposure or co-marketing for: Todoist, Moosend, Volza, WhatConverts, ElevenLabs, KrispCall, Setmore, or the standard affiliate track at MailerLite/Omnisend/Shopify/Wix.

This does not mean those vendors will never feature a high-performing affiliate. It means Miloosh must not represent reciprocal promotion as a contractual/current benefit until the vendor confirms it.

## Operating rule

Prioritize reciprocal visibility only when it is organic and does not require Miloosh to buy placement, tickets, certifications or services without owner approval. Performance-gated benefits should be converted into measurable referral targets rather than treated as current assets.
