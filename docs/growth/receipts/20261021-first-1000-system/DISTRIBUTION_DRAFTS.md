## Discussion answers (outlines, one per experiment; owner approves each thread, no link unless asked)
- **A:** in a thread asking how to choose between a prospecting database and LinkedIn: separate the price model (per seat plus credits vs per license), name the InMail cap, say what to check on the vendor pages (billing period, credit limits, minimum seats), offer the comparison only if the poster asks for sources.
- **B:** in a thread about a phone system for a small support team: say that headline prices are annual-term, list the monthly figures from the vendor pages, name the 12-month term where it applies, and what to confirm (included minutes, number fees, trial vs refund).
Never claim hands-on use, rankings or earnings; never mention a coupon; never post where a partner's rules forbid it (SurveyMonkey and Trainual restrict third-party social promotion; neither is used here).
