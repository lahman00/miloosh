# Google discovery stability — 2026-10-27

Status: IMPLEMENTED_LOCAL and TESTED locally. Not pushed, merged or deployed. No claim that this recovers Google traffic.

## Defect (confirmed)
`app/sitemap.ts`, the software page's related-comparison links and `scripts/maintenance/seo.ts` used `isSoftwareIndexingReady` / `isComparisonIndexingReady` at `new Date()`. Both embed a 90-day age test, so a page already submitted and linked would silently disappear when `accessedAt` or `pricing.lastVerified` crossed 90 days, with no content change. Airtable (2026-10-30), Intercom (2026-10-31) and Semrush (2026-11-03) were the first due. Frozen-date run of the new queue: 30 admitted products overdue by 2026-11-03, 77 by 2026-11-30, 187 by 2026-12-31 (all 265 are overdue or within 14 days by then).

## Change
Quality admission is now separate from fact re-verification.
- `data/seo/discovery-baseline.ts` (generated by `scripts/seo/capture-discovery-baseline.ts`): slugs only, plus capture time, source SHA and sitemap hash. It documents discovery continuity, not freshness.
- `lib/discovery-continuity.ts`: strict-eligible is always allowed. A baseline product with strict failures is retained only if every failure is `content-freshness` or `pricing-freshness` AND `accessedAt` and `pricing.lastVerified` are real, non-future `YYYY-MM-DD` dates. Any other reason (pricing evidence, constraints, source coverage, feature depth, buyer fit), a missing/malformed/impossible/future date, a product outside the baseline, or an invalid registry fails closed. A comparison needs both products eligible and, unless both are strict-ready, its exact ordered slug in the baseline. Two admitted products never admit an arbitrary pair.
- Wired into `app/sitemap.ts`, `app/software/[slug]/page.tsx` (same predicate for related links) and `scripts/maintenance/seo.ts`. `lib/indexing-quality.ts` and the strict diagnostics are untouched, so stale reasons stay visible. `lastmod`, priorities and visible HTML are unchanged.
- `lib/discovery-maintenance.ts` + `npm run seo:reverification-queue [-- --as-of=YYYY-MM-DD] [--json]`: read-only list of overdue/due-soon products with the official pricing source and re-verification steps. Never edits dates. Added as a report step in `maintenance.yml`.
- `npm run seo:release-sentinel` (also a step in `ci.yml`): exits 1 if any baseline URL is absent from `sitemap()` or the registry is invalid. The only exception is an exact-path entry with reason, evidence, reviewer and date in `data/seo/discovery-intentional-removals.ts` (empty now; no wildcards).

Not done, by design: no date bumps, no 90-day change, no gate disabled, no noindex or suppression change, no RSC robots change, no new pages, no product edits, Guardian untouched.

## Known risks
- Continuity has no expiry: a baseline page whose facts are never re-verified stays submitted indefinitely, with old prices. The queue makes this visible but a human must act; prices are not claimed current. Consider a reviewed maximum age.
- Page body freshness labels still reflect real dates; I did not check how visible copy reads for pages past 90 days.
- New products need current strict readiness; the baseline must be regenerated deliberately after a reviewed release.
- Retained URLs are a hint to Google, not a guarantee of indexing or traffic.
- Supervisor items: shared-template proof, independent review, release and production SHA confirmation.
