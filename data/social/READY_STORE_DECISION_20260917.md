## LinkedIn — נוסח מוצע באנגלית
A small catalog does not always mean a simple platform decision.

Twenty products tells you about the catalog. It does not tell you which checkout you can use, who maintains the store, or what the first year will cost.

Start with three checks:
1. Payments: confirm support for your business location and the payment methods your customers need.
2. Total cost: on Shopify, applicable third-party transaction fees are separate from your payment provider's processing fees. Check the plan, payment method and billing term, not one headline price.
3. Daily work: Wix is worth evaluating when managing the website and store together is the priority. Shopify is worth evaluating when selling and fulfilling orders is the central workload.

Then check product limits and growth requirements against the plan you would actually buy. Repairing an existing store or adding commerce to an existing site can also be the right decision.

## Facebook — נוסח מוצע באנגלית
Choosing a platform for a small store? Count more than products.

Before comparing plans, check:
• Can you accept the payments your customers need in your business location?
• What will the platform, required apps and payment processing actually cost?
• Do you mainly need a business website with a store, or a store built around selling and fulfilling orders?

Wix and Shopify deserve a look for different workloads. Keeping your current website or repairing your existing store is also a valid option.
