# Measurement Plan — Why We Don't Rank War Phase V

## What to check, and when

| Window | What to check |
|---|---|
| Day 0 (2026-09-26) | Baseline: 0 branded impressions (brand-demand.json), ~100 total external links with ~93 likely-infrastructure (authority-gap.json), narrow 45-79 median position band across categories (domain-pattern.json). |
| Day 28-60 | Re-run the exact same three checks (branded query filter, GSC Links report, category-position band). Structured-data enrichment (Organization schema) is a slow-moving trust signal -- it would be premature to expect any measurable change before at least a month. |
| Ongoing | If the user or Antigravity pursues real external growth (directory listings, genuine editorial mentions, social presence), re-check the Links report and branded-query volume after each real milestone to see whether the domain-level position band starts to loosen. |

## What this measurement plan is actually testing

Not a single page's ranking movement -- Part 9's domain-level hypothesis, which this session's
evidence supports but cannot fully prove causally. The real test is whether, as external
authority signals (backlinks, brand awareness) grow over time, the currently-narrow ~45-79
median-position band across unrelated categories starts to widen (some categories pulling
ahead of others as page-level factors start to matter more once the domain-level ceiling
lifts). That is a multi-month signal, not a Day-7/14/28 one -- flagged honestly rather than
promising a fast readout this specific hypothesis cannot deliver.

## What NOT to do

- Do not conclude domain trust is "fixed" from any single Day-28 snapshot -- this is a slow,
  cumulative signal.
- Do not manufacture backlinks or branded-search activity to move these numbers artificially --
  that would invalidate the measurement itself, not just violate the standing anti-spam rules.
