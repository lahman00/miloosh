Mode: Persuade. Scope: homepage, navigation, shared visual tokens and catalogue cards. Preserve content, URLs, affiliate behavior and source records.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Software discovery should feel like a thoughtful shortlist, not an endless inventory. Show what the reader can actually compare immediately.

OWN-WORLD: Ramp-informed warm light canvas, forest ink, citrine action, sage comparison desk; large Manrope typography and spare 1px rules. The existing M mark stays recognizable.

STORY: Understand software fit, inspect real options, then search or use the matcher. Retain evidence-ranked comparisons and all priority internal links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 80px navigation; 76px two-line headline and 56px search; an interactive software shortlist occupies the adjacent sage field. Catalogue evidence at the fold. Mobile stacks copy, search, then shortlist with 24px gutters.

FORM: User-pinned Ramp direction (approved through structured choice, no random seed). Code-led execution against the measured live reference, not stored as a standing workflow preference. Signature interaction: switch a real shortlist by use case with a 180ms active state and one subtle 650ms entrance.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
