# Miloosh PR Target Map — 2026-08-24

Purpose: earned-media target map for Miloosh's verified SaaS-pricing / software-cost research. This is not a mass-outreach list. Do not contact anyone until Miloosh has an original, reproducible finding with methodology and source rules.

## Core story

**Not:** “Miloosh launched.”

**Story:** SaaS pricing is becoming harder for buyers to forecast as vendors mix seats, credits, usage, outcomes, AI allowances, annual commitments and switching constraints. Miloosh can become a source of buyer-visible pricing evidence and change tracking.

### Pitch condition

Do not pitch generic commentary. Pitch only when Miloosh can provide at least one fresh, reproducible data point such as:

- share of verified products using seat vs usage/credit/outcome pricing
- share of sampled products where AI is included vs paid add-on vs metered
- share with free tier / time-limited free access
- median modeled cost for 5 / 10 / 25 / 50-seat teams within a clearly defined sample
- verified examples of plan/pricing changes with before/after evidence
- quantified switching constraints such as minimum seats / annual contract requirements where the dataset truly supports this

Every pitch must include sample size, verification window and methodology URL.

## Competitive research warning

Several 2026 pricing reports already exist, including research based on hundreds or thousands of products / buyers. Miloosh cannot win by publishing generic statements such as “AI pricing is becoming usage-based.” That is already known.

Miloosh needs a differentiated data advantage such as:

1. **Buyer-verifiable source trail** for every pricing fact.
2. **Change tracking**: what changed, when, and source proof.
3. **Switching implications** tied to pricing changes.
4. **Comparable team-cost scenarios** from live public pricing.
5. **Free-tier erosion / time-limited-free tracking.**
6. **Hidden constraints**: minimum seats, annual commitments, credits, overages, add-ons.

## First PR data product to build

### Miloosh SaaS Pricing Pressure Index 2026

Minimum publishable methodology:

- define the software sample before calculating results
- state sample size
- state collection / verification dates
- use first-party vendor sources where available
- separate observed fact from modeled cost
- do not infer historical change without archived prior evidence
- publish inclusion/exclusion rules
- publish how missing/unverifiable prices are handled
- version the dataset/report so updates are auditable

Candidate dimensions:

| Dimension | Type |
|---|---|
| Free tier present | observed |
| Free tier time-limited | observed |
| Starting paid price | observed |
| Pricing unit | observed |
| AI included | observed |
| AI add-on / credits / usage meter | observed |
| Minimum seats | observed |
| Annual commitment required | observed |
| Last verified | observed |
| 5/10/25/50-seat modeled cost | calculated, only when mathematically valid |
| Pricing changed since prior verified snapshot | historical, only with real prior evidence |
