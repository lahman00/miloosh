# Original-Data Opportunity — Re-Check (2026-09-27)

## What was built tonight

**The CMS Buying Decision Matrix 2026** (`/research/cms-buying-decision-2026`) -- the fourth
research asset. Chosen over the other three candidates named in this mission
(AI voice commercial-use rights, email-marketing automation gates, project-management seat/guest
rules) because:

- **Coverage**: all 8 CMS products already have (or, for Webflow, could be verified live tonight)
  real primary-source pricing and migration data -- the strongest coverage of any candidate.
- **Source quality**: every claim traces to a specific vendor page, with honest `unknownFields`
  where a fact could not be confirmed (e.g. Umbraco's support pricing, blocked by Cloudflare
  during tonight's fetch attempts).
- **Buyer usefulness**: migration lock-in is a real, high-stakes, underserved question -- every
  vendor markets how easy it is to switch TO them; almost none document switching away.
- **Citation potential**: confirmed tonight via the link-earning research below -- multiple real,
  existing blogs (Capterra, agency blogs, vendor blogs) already publish CRM/CMS feature-gating and
  pricing content with no cited primary source, exactly the gap this kind of dataset fills.
- **SERP relevance**: directly serves the Craft CMS, Umbraco, and Drupal query families already
  identified as winnable-or-monitored in this session's SERP map.
